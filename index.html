FAQ

Before you join.

Still stuck? Ask in the free #start-here channel and someone from the team will answer.

Do you take bets or hold my money?

No. We teach and share analysis. You place any bets yourself, on licensed sportsbooks that are legal where you live.

How old do I need to be?

You must be 21 or older (or the legal betting age in your state) to join the betting channels. The TikTok Shop playbook follows TikTok's own age rules.

Will I make money?

Nobody can promise that, and you should be wary of anyone who does. Betting always carries risk of loss, and TikTok Shop income depends on the work you put in. We show the methods and post our results openly, wins and losses.

What is +EV betting?

Positive expected value betting means only placing a bet when the sportsbook's odds pay more than the true chance of the outcome justifies. One bet can still lose; the edge shows up over hundreds of bets with disciplined sizing.

Do I need money to start TikTok Shop?

Very little. The affiliate route means you promote other brands' products for commission, so there is no inventory to buy. Some AI tools have paid tiers, and we show free options first.

How do I cancel or switch plans?

From your Whop account, any time. Upgrades apply right away. If you cancel, you keep access until the end of the billing period, then your Discord role is removed.

Do you offer refunds?

Because you get instant access to all content, payments are non-refundable. Message us within 48 hours if something went wrong with your access and we will fix it.

Which sports do the alerts cover?

Whatever is in season and has an edge: NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, college football and basketball, and major soccer leagues.
